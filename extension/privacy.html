Privacy Policy for KivoAI

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

Overview

KivoAI is designed with a privacy-first approach. It operates as an autonomous browser assistant powered by local or self-hosted artificial intelligence models.

Data Collection and Usage

KivoAI does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any centralized third-party servers. Specifically:

  • AI Processing: Visual data (screenshots) and web page content are sent exclusively to your configured Open WebUI server endpoint to perform requested tasks. No data is harvested or retained by external entities.
  • Autofill Profiles: Profile details (such as names, addresses, and emails) are stored locally within your browser's secure storage (`chrome.storage.local`) and are never shared or transmitted anywhere except directly into form fields you instruct the agent to fill.
  • Audio Recordings: Voice prompts are captured temporarily solely for transcription purposes and are discarded immediately after processing.

Permissions

KivoAI requests only the minimum necessary permissions required to function, such as executing scripts to interact with web pages and accessing your microphone for voice dictation when explicitly triggered.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within this document.
